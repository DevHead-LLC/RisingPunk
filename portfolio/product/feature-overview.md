# Feature Overview

RisingPunk combined **tycoon / financial strategy** with **async hacker warfare** on a shared world map.

## Core loops

### Turf (base)

- Home, Digital Barracks, Research Center, Rental Housing, Bot Assembly
- Passive income, construction timers, research unlocks
- Guided tasks and daily haul mini-game

### Hack map (world)

- Large grid map with player bases, NPCs, and teammates
- **Async attack marches** — launch bots, travel time, headless PvP/NPC battles while offline
- Probes, shields, crew tools, world chat
- Battle reports delivered as private messages with replay support

### Crews & social

- Create/join hack crews, crew chat, rankings
- **Swarm (group hack)** — coordinated multi-player attacks
- Private messaging with moderation and admin tools
- Peer **transfer runs** — send items/cash to crewmates (fee + march timing)

### Bug hunt

- Ant instances on the map, hunter facility (Kaito), token economy
- Bug-hunt marches, storage items, travel speedups
- Underground Exchange in-game shop (speedups, token packs)

### Battles

- Grid-based tactical battles (live and replay)
- Battalion assignment, presets, NPC and PvP outcomes
- XP, wallet rewards, bot losses, battle replay viewer

### Monetization

- In-app purchases (developer support tiers) via App Store / Play Billing
- Server-side receipt verification and ledger
