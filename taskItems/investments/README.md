# RisingPunk Fundraising OS

A step-by-step doc set for angel and game-focused investor conversations. Work through these in order so your story, metrics, and ask are aligned before outreach.

**Tone:** Confident but honest. No hype. No MBA jargon.

**Signal:** We are explicitly pursuing **pre-monetization, patient capital** to reach critical mass in a freemium MMO. The docs filter for the right investors and repel short-term ROI expectations.

## How to Use This (Steps in Order)

### Phase 1: Know Your Story

1. **[1_constructed_metrics.md](1_constructed_metrics.md)**  
   Define your 1–2 primary metrics and how you talk about early signals. Do this first so you never sound uncertain on traction.

2. **[2_product_loop_and_retention.md](2_product_loop_and_retention.md)**  
   Lock in the core loop and retention hooks (PvP, crews, bots, economy). This is what game investors care about most.

3. **[3_monetization_strategy_future.md](3_monetization_strategy_future.md)**  
   Clarify F2P philosophy, planned IAP categories, and why monetization is deferred. Shows you understand it without overclaiming.

4. **[4_risk_and_unknowns.md](4_risk_and_unknowns.md)**  
   List known risks and unknowns, plus mitigation. Do this before raising so you’re not caught off guard.

### Phase 2: Know Your Ask

5. **[5_funding_use_of_proceeds.md](5_funding_use_of_proceeds.md)**  
   Define range (min / comfortable / max), allocation, and milestones. Tie money to outcomes.

6. **[6_ownership_and_diligence_readiness.md](6_ownership_and_diligence_readiness.md)**  
   Clean ownership and paperwork: Apple/App Store under company, transfer plan, business bank, DBA. So when someone wants to write a check, you can close without friction.

### Phase 3: Find and Talk to Them

7. **[7_investor_outreach_targets.md](7_investor_outreach_targets.md)**  
   Build your target list: ideal profile, categories, where to find them, and a simple CRM template.

8. **[8_investor_conversation_framework.md](8_investor_conversation_framework.md)**  
   Use your 30-second overview, 2–3 minute deepening, prepared Q&A, and explicit ask. Prevents rambling and self-undermining.
